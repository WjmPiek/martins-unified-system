Compass aggregation: 100 m constrained people-count grid aggregated to a
500 m grid; application polygon intersections use 4 by 4 cell samples.
Coverage: South African mainland; see manifest for excluded offshore areas.
These are modelled estimates, not recorded custom-territory census counts.

territory_reference.json contains numerical facts from Stats SA Census 2022
in Brief, Table 3.1 and Figure 4.1. They are historical provincial context,
not current household measurements for arbitrary polygons.

The 2024 mortality/provincial denominator reference was imported from the
existing Compass model. Until live verification succeeds it is explicitly
marked cached_unverified with low confidence, not a newly verified retrieval.

The source adapters retain publisher links and dates. OSM geography and roads
must retain OpenStreetMap contributor attribution. Census 2011 boundaries are
credited to Stats SA / UCT Libraries and retain their vintage.

Official municipality and ward boundaries are attributed to the Municipal
Demarcation Board (MDB). Publisher metadata and release links are retained
with each selected part. MDB permits research, publications and value-added
applications with attribution; do not resell or pass modified geography off
as an original MDB product. Combined franchise outlines are derived planning
areas and require separate confirmation against the franchise agreement.
Source: https://dataportal-mdb-sa.opendata.arcgis.com/
