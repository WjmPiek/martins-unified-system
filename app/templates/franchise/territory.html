{% if title=='Demand and service mix' %}
How demand and service income are calculated

Annual resident deaths = selected population × annual mortality rate ÷ 1,000. Monthly franchise services = annual deaths ÷ 12 × market share ÷ 100. Funeral income uses the burial/cremation mix and entered cash package prices, with the selected ramp-up and annual growth assumptions.

{% elif title=='Operating costs and income' %}
How operating income and expenses are calculated

Net policy administration income = premiums collected − insurer payover. Business income adds funeral services, other sales and any additional policy commission. Manual operating costs add direct costs, other fixed costs, owner salary and contractual royalties. Percentage mode uses the total expense percentage, including royalties and owner salary. Territory repayments, tax and reserves are deducted afterwards.

Optional Territory Fee — calculation details

Fee = the lower of the five-year profit-margin valuation and the affordable fee ceiling. Population, expected resident deaths, achievable market share, service mix and costs drive the forecast. A geographically larger territory carries no automatic premium.

For each year, multiply projected operating profit by that year's profit margin, then add the five contributions. R1,000,000 turnover and R200,000 profit gives a 20% margin and a R40,000 contribution. A loss or non-positive turnover contributes R0.

Profit includes operating costs and monthly royalties, before tax, reserves, financing and territory repayments. Turnover is forecast business income, excluding insurer payover. Existing volume, price and cost growth assumptions apply.

The affordable ceiling tests monthly liquidity, owner salary coverage, five-year investment recovery, the entered target margin and a 20% lower-sales scenario with the entered safety buffer. It includes entered tax, reserves, finance charges and repayment terms; zero or omitted costs remain assumptions requiring review. The ceiling is rounded down to a whole Rand.

This is an owner-requested planning calculation, not a verified FASA-prescribed formula.
